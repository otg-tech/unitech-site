3. Cookies and similar technologies

The website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, tracking pixels or advertising tools. Local times for our regions are calculated in your browser and are not sent to us.

4. Service providers

We use a small number of providers who process data on our behalf or as independent controllers:

  • GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages) hosts the website and processes technical request data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect it.
  • Google LLC (Google Fonts) delivers the fonts used on the website. Your browser requests the fonts directly from Google, which receives your IP address and browser information.
  • Microsoft Corporation (Microsoft 365) provides our e-mail service and stores our correspondence.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with third parties for their marketing. We may disclose personal data where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside the United Arab Emirates and outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. We rely on providers that apply recognised safeguards for international transfers, such as contractual data protection terms and, where applicable, the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep data

  • Technical request data is kept by our hosting provider for the limited period set out in its own policies.
  • Correspondence is kept for as long as needed to handle your request and any resulting cooperation, and then deleted, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
  • Contract-related data is kept for the period required by applicable accounting, tax and corporate law.

7. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you have the right to:

  • be informed about and access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected and incomplete data completed;
  • have your data deleted when it is no longer needed or was processed unlawfully;
  • restrict or object to processing, including processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • receive your data in a portable format, where applicable;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority, including the UAE Data Office or, if you are in the European Economic Area, the supervisory authority in your country.

To exercise your rights, write to info@unitech.capital. We may ask you to confirm your identity and will respond within the time limits set by applicable law.

8. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) to the website and access controls for our e-mail and internal systems. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to keep risks low.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services or legal requirements change. The current version is always published on this page with its effective date. If we add new ways of collecting data, such as an online chat or contact form, we will update this policy before they go live.
