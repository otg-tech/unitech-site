How a connected warehouse works

  2. 2

  3. 3

Challenges and solutions

From challenge to solution

AI brings new demand and a new pace of work. For each challenge it creates, we offer a concrete answer.

  1. Challenge

    Not enough fiber

    AI data centers use 36× more fiber than regular racks. New capacity takes up to two years to build. TrendForce, STL

    Our solution

    More good cable from existing lines: fewer defects and better planning.

  2. Challenge

    Quality must keep up with speed

    When output grows, manual checks miss defects and slow the line down.

    Our solution

    Cameras check products on the line, and AI warns about problems before defects appear.

  3. Challenge

    Warehouse data is out of date

    Stock records do not match reality, and every recount stops work.

    Our solution

    LiDAR, RFID and cameras keep stock data current without stopping work.

  4. Challenge

    AI moves faster than companies

    New tools appear every month, while knowledge stays in files and people’s heads.

    Our solution

    Knowledge base, document automation and staff training as one programme.
